I have been struggling with limiting the screen-on time on my phone. I am addicted to instagram reels, YouTube, and especially Twitter. 
These website (with exception of YT) are highly negative and toxic these days for me - I have tried to tune the X homepage and it has only slightly helped. But it is very negative - maybe reflects the true thinking of the world. 

Anywhichways, I want to reduce the usage. I have tried many techniques and apps (like app timer) but they haven't helped. I just go past those barriers and still use these. Today, a new idea hit me. 

I am already on a quest to improve my fitness. I thought why not let my brain have all the dopamine it wants - but the only catch is - I have to be standing, walking, or exercising to keep using it. So my brain has to expend caloric energy to actually enjoy the dopamine. 

This is a new thing - I hope it works unlike my other initiatives/battles against this addiction. I'll check back in sometime in the future. Hope this sticks!
